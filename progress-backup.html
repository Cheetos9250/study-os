Important limitations

This is a read-only backup, not a restore tool. It does not overwrite your existing progress.

This backup excludes Study Planner records in IndexedDB and records stored only in Cloudflare or Notion. Export those separately when their tools are available. The original textbook PDF is not included; keep your own copy and select it again in the reader when needed.

Before clearing Safari, changing devices, or upgrading, save and check the file in Files/iCloud Drive. Do not delete your original progress. If you use an iPad Home Screen app, launch this page from that same app: its storage may be separate from Safari.
