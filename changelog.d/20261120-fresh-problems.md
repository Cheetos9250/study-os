Accounting practice: new **Fresh problems** chapter. Random-number questions (purchases with returns and discount, net sales, net cost of purchases, gross profit, weighted average, FIFO, allowance for doubtful accounts, note interest, bank reconciliation) made in the browser by `scripts/acc-generator.js`; the options are shuffled and every wrong option is a typical mistake. **New problems** makes a fresh set. Their answers are not kept: only one batch of `gen-*` ids is ever in `study-acc`.
