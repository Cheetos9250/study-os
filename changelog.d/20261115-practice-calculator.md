- Practice calculator: a **Calc** button on the quiz pages (Accounting, Economics, Math → Practice) and on Accounting Textbook Exercises opens a calculator you can drag anywhere. It has + − × ÷, %, brackets, delete-last and clear, shows the running result with thousands separators, and keeps the last 4 results as buttons you can tap to reuse. Works with a keyboard too (numbers, operators, Enter, Backspace, Esc). It does plain arithmetic (no eval) and stores nothing.
